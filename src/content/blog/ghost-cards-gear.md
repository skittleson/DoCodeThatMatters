I'm doing a startup!  Yup, I have a niche idea that that solves a real problem that I truly care about it and I think others will as well.  This is not a promotional blog post (you could consider it if you want but then leave), my focus is doing something that Candy Japan did which was to document at least the first steps of a product creation, costs, and difficulties in the setup. Leave if you want!

## The story of many

There has been multiple times where I left my phone, dropped my phone, go to a remove location where AT&T doesnt work or the battery has died. I usually cant remember any phone numbers so i end up calling two numbers i know off hand but sometimes they dont pick up.  It happens, it happens to many people.  The approach i've been doing for awhile is to have a piece of paper in my wallet with emergency contacts. The ink can smear, fade, and can be torn.  

## Solutions

I solved the problem for myself in a few ways.  No tear paper and ink. That worked for a bit! i liked it but it wasnt the most wallet friendly but good enough behind the phone.  It was good enough for when I needed it.  I also noticed same problem for much of family and friends.  I told them of the trick. Some did, some didnt until it was too late.  Came down to few factors of cost, time, and effort to prepare. Also, kids dont care wallets (surprise!). 

So back to board. Then after researching for a bit I remembered there is 3d printing letters like stencils. its no ink, hard to break.  I 3d printed one myself (even with a few sd micro card slots). It was more then good enough for wallet solution and behind the phone solution.

I've used a few times now and have printed a ton for family members with their own unique information on it.


### Too many requests so automation had to happen

Since i was printing this all the time.  I wanted some approaches that could automate my workflow:

1. Basic information captured
2. Automation of the 3D printed STLs
3. Automated of of gcode files sliced from STLS

That was mostly easy. 


##  Why jump into a business around it?

I see some companies doing similar things but not like this.  It's a bit Every Day Care aka EDC and safety net. I worry about my family and this makes me worry less.
